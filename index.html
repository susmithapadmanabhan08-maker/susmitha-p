Mindset

How I Think

  • Who am I designing for?

    Start with people, not screens.

  • What problem are they facing?

    Name the real difficulty.

  • What's the simplest solution?

    Remove until it's clear.

  • Does it actually make things easier?

    Check it, then improve it.
